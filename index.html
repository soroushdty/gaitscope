      MATLAB .mat files saved by MATLAB or Octave (v5 to v7, and v7.3, which loads a 41 KB reader the first time), like the course’s Walking.mat. Any variable name works. Samples should run down the rows; a time column first is optional.

      Physics Toolbox .csv exports from the G-Force Meter, Linear Accelerometer, Gyroscope or Multi Record, with elapsed or clock time, comma or semicolon separated.

      phyphox .zip exports (Export data → CSV) of Acceleration with g or without g, as phyphox saves them, or the Raw Data.csv inside. The phone, sensor and start time from its meta folder are listed with the checks.

      Other CSV files with numbers in columns, with or without a header row.
